01 / WHY IT MATTERED

Why it mattered

Authentic, naturally written business documents have a different texture from generated demonstrations. The project sought real human-authored .docx material representing the documents people actually create at work. In the work described in my earlier project notes, AI-generated submissions were prohibited.

  • Preserve ordinary business document structure and writing style
  • Select appropriate document types and verify the source material
  • Respect project authenticity criteria
02 / HOW I WORKED

How I worked

I reviewed document suitability, structural coherence, enterprise relevance and privacy exposure. I worked with business/communications and finance examples, checking whether titles, numbers, tables, labels and supporting sections remained consistent and usable.

  • Business-document quality assurance
  • Financial/operational report checks, including reconciliation and percentage consistency
  • Cross-page layout review and evidence coherence
03 / PRIVACY, IDENTITY AND METADATA

Privacy, identity and metadata

The assignment involved handling PII and BII. I removed identifiers such as names, contact information, organization references, logos and identifying partner details, while also considering document properties and hidden metadata. This was done to protect sensitive information without unnecessarily damaging the document’s functional value.

  • Visible PII/BII review and sanitization
  • Document properties and metadata checks
  • File integrity and layout verification
04 / THE 19-PAGE REVIEW

The 19-page review

One documented communications-strategy workflow involved editing and rechecking a 19-page Word document, preserving the expected document appearance while removing sensitive references. I used the render to verify that formatting and structure were intact. An earlier recorded submission received 100% average scoring; that is a project-feedback observation, not an overall project-wide accuracy rate.

  • Document structure and consistency checks
  • Render-based visual QA
  • Iterative corrections after reviewing output
05 / WHAT RECRUITERS SHOULD SEE

What recruiters should see

This is a strong example of methodical document QA, responsible data-handling, metadata hygiene and written judgement. It complements AI evaluation because document-based model training and enterprise automation depend on the quality of the source documents.

  • Word-document inspection
  • Privacy-conscious quality assurance
  • Detailed analytical review
