Professional practice / Methodology

Metadata & structured-output QA

Evaluating whether model-generated structure is valid, complete and faithful to the original instructions.

01 / METHOD

What I reviewed

I checked field types, required and optional keys, nesting, null and missing-value handling, conditional rules and metadata alignment against the task and expected schema.

02 / METHOD

How I diagnosed errors

I distinguished a formatting failure from a factual failure and tested whether the error came from the model response, the instruction or an overly rigid validation check.

03 / METHOD

Professional value

A response can be readable yet unusable downstream. This work illustrates my ability to translate abstract rules into repeatable data-quality checks.

This study describes methods and responsibilities from supplied experience. It does not reproduce confidential client tasks or claim unverified project outcomes.
