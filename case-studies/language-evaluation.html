Professional practice / Methodology

English-language evaluation

How a technically correct response can still fail in clarity, tone, register, naturalness or audience fit.

01 / METHOD

The evaluation task

Across model-review assignments, I assessed whether generated English was grammatically sound, natural, clear, relevant and appropriate for the intended audience.

02 / METHOD

What I looked for

I checked UK and US English conventions, inappropriate register, awkward wording, excessive verbosity, misleading certainty and failures to match the requested tone.

03 / METHOD

Why it matters

Language quality affects trust and usability. My strength is connecting detailed editorial judgement with consistent task criteria and written justifications.

This study describes methods and responsibilities from supplied experience. It does not reproduce confidential client tasks or claim unverified project outcomes.
