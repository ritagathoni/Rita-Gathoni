Turing

Turing is the clearest example of volume meeting judgement. The work required reviewing hundreds of outputs while staying careful, consistent and accountable in the face of ambiguity.

RoleLLM Evaluation Domain Expert

Evaluated model outputs across text, structured and agent-related tasks in a distributed remote workflow.

FocusQuality at scale

The challenge was keeping evaluations sharp and reproducible even at high throughput.

What it provesConsistency

I can operate at scale without reducing complex judgement to superficial scoring.

01 / Scope

What the work involved

At Turing, I evaluated more than 400 outputs per week across reasoning, structured outputs, prompt testing, multimodal work and agent-related tasks. I reviewed factuality, instruction-following, clarity, safety, schema compliance and whether responses would be usable downstream.

02 / What I brought to the work

Judgement, not just throughput

I stress-tested prompts, reviewed edge cases, checked JSON and field logic, examined tool-use behaviour and turned repeated failure patterns into written notes that could support calibration or QA. That work matters because scale creates a temptation to reduce everything to speed. My contribution was maintaining discernment.

Supplied career materials cite approximately 97% evaluation quality, around 15% improvement in reviewer consistency across recurring patterns and roughly 20% gain in repetitive validation efficiency. These are presented as qualified estimates from the supplied materials.
03 / Common failure modes

Where models regularly struggled

Unsupported claims, missing steps, weak task completion, malformed structured outputs, incomplete tool arguments and answers that sounded correct while quietly failing the actual instruction. I worked to distinguish these patterns so the feedback could be used, not just archived.

04 / Why this case matters

The strongest signal

Turing shows the strongest combination of scale, technical detail and repeatable judgement in my portfolio. It also brings together several strands of my work: prompt testing, quality assurance, structured output review and clear written rationales.
