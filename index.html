01 / MODEL QUALITY
RULE≠RESULTVerify the verifier

Alibaba / Qwen - the case that proved the judge can fail too

This project stands out because it was not just about rating model outputs. It required checking whether the evaluation logic itself was valid. I worked through business rules, dates, ETAs, approval paths, conditional reasoning and structured outputs, then distinguished model failures from verifier failures.

What made it difficult

Correct answers could still be marked wrong when the verifier logic was incomplete or misaligned with the underlying policy.

What I did

Reviewed source evidence, traced calculation paths, checked assumptions, and rebuilt the rationale behind each check.

Why it matters

It demonstrates a higher-order skill: evaluating not only the answer, but the evaluation system itself.

Key themes

Qwen outputs, metadata alignment, business logic, verifier QA, deterministic calculations, and careful written reasoning.

02 / AGENT BEHAVIOUR
NAVIGATION→ACTIONReconstruct the journey

Project Blue Dog - watching agents work in the real world

Blue Dog focused on AI agents interacting with real websites. I assessed how well they navigated, whether their interactions were sensible, how efficiently they completed a task and whether their final outputs were actually supported by what happened during the task.

Evaluation dimensions

Navigation, interaction, efficiency and output quality formed the backbone of the work.

My contribution

Followed action trails and screenshots, separated technical blockers from judgement errors, and documented where the agent’s workflow broke down.

Why it matters

Agent evaluation demands more than reading a final answer. It requires reconstructing behaviour.

Skills shown

Multimodal review, web research, evidence tracing, and comparative assessment of agent performance.
